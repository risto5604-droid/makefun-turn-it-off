One funny line a hopeless tech support agent says to a desperate customer. A house judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier, fresher, more agent-true line each time.

## The entry

> Your files are gone. My entire toolkit is asking you to turn it off and on again.

## Why it works

The customer opens with real desperation — "Your files are gone" — and the agent answers with cheerful, total uselessness: the most famous support question in the world, "have you tried turning it off and on again?", is not one trick in the agent's toolbox, it *is* the toolbox. The classic line is twisted from a fix into the agent's only tool, so the joke lands on the agent's empty toolkit, never on the customer. The customer's desperation is felt in four words, not explained — and the customer is never the butt of the joke.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
